Established in 2006, Triomada Plastic International Co. is the leading Stretch Films manufacturer in the Middle East region. With state-of-the-art manufacturing in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, sales offices in 10 countries and clients in over 40 countries, our films are widely used for automatic, semi-automatic, inline wrapping machines, as well as for hand application.
